Dumb-Proof Rules

If it says ENTER LONG: buy near the entry, set stop immediately, set take profit immediately.
If it says WAIT: do not force a trade. No trade is a valid decision.
If it says BLOCKED: spread, liquidity, or timing is unsafe. Do not enter manually.
